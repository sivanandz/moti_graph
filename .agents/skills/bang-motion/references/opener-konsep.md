# Opener & Promo Concepts — 19 Structural Archetypes

To ensure no two promotional videos or openers share identical structure, select from this menu of 19 tested concepts.

---

## 1. The Structure Fingerprint

Before writing code for an opener or promo, document its unique **Structure Fingerprint**:
`[Concept Name] · [Number of Scenes] · [Hero Object per Scene] · [Opening Hook] · [Signature Climax] · [Brand Lockup]`

Two projects must never share the same fingerprint.

---

## 2. The 19 Opener Concepts

### 1. Slam & Reveal
- **Structure**: Giant kinetic headline slams into frame → typography fractures as the hero physical product bursts through the center → camera decelerates into an elegant product glide.
- **Best For**: High-impact brand launches, event teasers, bold announcements.

### 2. Microscopic Zoom
- **Structure**: Extreme macro close-up of a single texture or micro-component → camera pulls back rapidly in exponential stages, revealing the complete architecture.
- **Best For**: Engineering products, premium hardware, precision software.

### 3. Workflow Chain
- **Structure**: Step 1 (Input/Raw State) → Step 2 (Automated Processing/Transformation) → Step 3 (Flawless Output). The camera follows the continuous data pipeline.
- **Best For**: B2B SaaS, developer tools, productivity suites.

### 4. Split Contrast
- **Structure**: Screen bisected down the center. Left side represents the chaotic old reality; right side shows the streamlined modern solution. The divider slides dynamically to consume the frame.
- **Best For**: Problem/solution explainers, speed comparisons.

### 5. Blueprint Assembly
- **Structure**: Fine white/cyan CAD wireframe schematic drawn on a dark cutting mat → parts fly together and lock into place → schematic renders into a photorealistic textured 3D asset.
- **Best For**: Deep tech, architecture, scientific instrumentation.

### 6. The Continuous Line
- **Structure**: A single unbroken kinetic vector stroke loops through 3D space, outlining features, framing text, and finally forming the brand logo.
- **Best For**: Design-centric brands, creative software, agile platforms.

### 7. Data Surge
- **Structure**: Rapid cascade of metrics, real-time counters, and ascending telemetry graphs culminate at the core breakthrough claim.
- **Best For**: Fintech, analytics platforms, quantitative tools.

### 8. Card Shuffle
- **Structure**: A 3D deck of feature cards rapidly flicks forward toward the camera, each card landing with physics bounce before being discarded for the next.
- **Best For**: Consumer apps, e-commerce, multi-feature services.

### 9. Isometric Engine
- **Structure**: Exploded 30° axonometric view showing internal gears, circuits, or service modules operating harmoniously.
- **Best For**: Cloud infrastructure, logistics, complex platforms.

### 10. Typographic Wall
- **Structure**: Wall of dense scrolling kinetic typography with negative space forming the silhouette of the hero product.
- **Best For**: Fashion, music, cultural campaigns, manifestos.

### 11. Device Portal
- **Structure**: Realistic retro or modern hardware frame (CRT, smartphone, terminal) acts as a living portal to alternate digital dimensions.
- **Best For**: Media apps, retro games, creative software.

### 12. Kinetic Colorblock
- **Structure**: Saturated constructivist blocks of solid color punch in on the musical beat, reframing high-contrast editorial typography.
- **Best For**: Creative agencies, cultural festivals, product drops.

### 13. Minimal Spotlight
- **Structure**: Deep dark stage illuminated by a sweeping directional beam of light that catches metallic edges and highlights.
- **Best For**: Luxury goods, automotive, audio hardware.

### 14. Modular Grid
- **Structure**: Dynamic Swiss design grid expands, collapses, and rearranges tiles in fluid syncopated rhythm.
- **Best For**: Design systems, collaboration tools, enterprise platforms.

### 15. Liquid Wipe
- **Structure**: Fluid organic shapes wash across the screen with soft displacement, transitioning scenes with high elegance.
- **Best For**: Health, beverage, lifestyle, skincare.

### 16. Architectural Cut
- **Structure**: Monumental geometric forms shift along perspective vanishing points like brutalist architectural monoliths.
- **Best For**: Real estate, architecture, structural engineering.

### 17. Terminal Sequence
- **Structure**: Monospace CLI prompt initiates a command sequence, generating live telemetry blocks that expand into full graphical interfaces.
- **Best For**: AI developer tools, cybersecurity, serverless platforms.

### 18. Prism Refraction
- **Structure**: Clean ray of light strikes a transparent geometric lens, refracting into brilliant spectral chromatic dispersions.
- **Best For**: AI models, optics, creative rendering engines.

### 19. Velocity Loop
- **Structure**: Relentless forward camera momentum traveling through an infinite tunnel of milestones, ending at the brand horizon.
- **Best For**: Speed announcements, year-in-review reels, campaign climaxes.
